For the H200 hydrogen-powered UAV, my work focused on **optimising the integration of the fuselage-wing fairing** and evaluating the interaction between the propeller and the wing. The project aimed to reduce aerodynamic drag, improve overall stability, and ensure smooth airflow across all critical surfaces.

<!-- Recommended Image: Illustration of the H200 UAV showing the fuselage-wing fairing and propeller integration -->

---

### Problem Definition and Technical Requirements

* **Fuselage-Wing Integration:** Optimise the fairing geometry to minimise drag caused by interference between fuselage and wing.
* **Propeller Interaction:** Analyse propeller-induced flow effects on the wing to ensure stability and efficiency.
* **Simulation Verification:** Evaluate the initial design with both **Moving Reference Frame (MRF)** and **transient simulations** for validation.

---

### Process, Design, and Methodology

* **CAD & Fairing Design:** Developed fairing geometry in Siemens NX to ensure smooth fuselage-wing transition.
* **Aerodynamic Simulation:** Conducted simulations using **STAR-CCM+**, applying stacionary solvers (RANS) to capture detailed flow behaviour.
* **Iteration Workflow:** Generated geometry surfaces via Siemens NX, iterated designs, and validated results in CFD software for each refinement cycle.

---

### Results and Conclusions

* **Aerodynamic Improvement:** Achieved approximately **3% reduction in interference drag** on the fuselage-wing junction.
* **Key Competency:** Demonstrated proficiency in **CFD validation, MATLAB integration for design iteration**, and **mechanical design of aerodynamic fairings**.
