### 1. Executive Summary

During my time in the **Horus UPV aerodynamics team**, I contributed to the **H110 - Falco aircraft** project, focusing on **aerodynamic stability analysis and winglet optimisation**.  
The key goal was to **improve the aircraft's lift-to-drag ratio and stability** by integrating novel winglets and analysing the impact of weight distribution on aerodynamic performance.

<!--*Suggested image:* Diagram of H110 showing winglets and overall aircraft geometry.-->

---

### 2. Problem Definition and Technical Requirements

- **Aerodynamic Stability:** Evaluate the effects of weight changes on the center of gravity (CG) relative to the aerodynamic center, affecting the aircraft's pitch and roll behaviour.  
- **Drag Reduction:** Optimise winglets to reduce drag without compromising lift.  
- **Integration:** Ensure modifications fit within existing aircraft geometry and mission requirements.

---

### 3. Process, Design, and Methodology

- **Winglet Design:** Generated a point grid of the wing and winglet geometry using **MATLAB**, iterated the design based on drag reduction criteria.  
- **Aerodynamic Analysis:** Conducted simulations with software AVL to evaluate **time constants of damping and mode stability**.  
- **Validation:** Compared results against the original wing (without winglet) to quantify a **20% drag reduction**.  
- **Integration Checks:** Verified that winglet modifications adhered to structural and dimensional constraints of the H110.

### 4. Technical Results and Conclusions

- **Drag Reduction:** Achieved a measurable decrease in drag (~20%) with winglet implementation.  
- **Improved Stability:** Weight and CG adjustments allowed better control over pitch and roll damping.  
- **Key Competency:** Demonstrated skills in **aerodynamic optimisation, stability analysis, and MATLAB-driven design workflows**.
