### 1. Executive Summary

Within the **Horus UPV student team**, I contributed to the aerodynamic optimisation of the H101 Hermes UAV.  
My focus was on **rear fuselage improvements** to reduce flow detachment, improve drag, and enhance overall aerodynamic performance.  
This was my first major project in UAV aerodynamics, allowing me to apply **CAD design and flow evaluation** techniques.

---

### 2. Problem Definition and Technical Requirements

- **Drag Reduction:** Identify areas at the rear fuselage where flow separation occurred and implement design modifications.  
- **Flow Attachment:** Ensure smooth transition of the airflow to reduce vortices and parasitic drag.  
- **Integration Constraints:** Maintain the overall fuselage structure and ensure manufacturability within the student team resources.

---

### 3. Process, Design, and Methodology

- **CAD Modelling:** Designed rear fuselage adjustments using CAD to smooth contours and reduce separation zones.  
- **Flow Evaluation:** Analysed detachment and vorticity using CFD software (OpenFOAM).  
- **Iteration:** Adjusted geometry iteratively based on observed airflow patterns to improve aerodynamic efficiency.

### 4. Technical Results and Conclusions

- **Flow Improvement:** Reduced rear fuselage drag by eliminating separation zones (qualitative improvement).  
- **Key Learning:** Applied first principles of flow attachment and drag reduction in UAV aerodynamics.  
- **Design Competency:** Developed skills in CAD-based aerodynamic optimisation and iterative design processes.
