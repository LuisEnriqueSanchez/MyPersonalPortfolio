### 1. Executive Summary

This project at Zeleros involved the **complete mechanical design and system integration** of a high-density **21 kWh battery** for high-performance electric vehicles. My role included **module layout design, harness routing, and full integration of subsystems** developed by other teams.

### 2. Problem Definition and Technical Requirements

The battery design had to meet several critical constraints:

* **Electrical Isolation:** Ensure safe distances between high-voltage components.
* **Mechanical Integrity:** Guarantee structural support for cells and modules under vibration and impact loads.
* **Integration:** Harmoniously combine power, control, and cooling subsystems within strict packaging limits.
* **Maintainability:** Facilitate assembly, disassembly, and future maintenance.

---

### 3. Process, Design, and Methodology

* **CAD Layout:** Designed the battery housing, modules, and cable routing using Siemens NX.
* **Integration:** Coordinated closely with electrical and thermal teams to ensure full subsystem compatibility.
* **Validation:** Performed static mechanical checks to ensure structural integrity under operational and assembly conditions.
* **Routing & Harnessing:** Optimised harness paths to reduce length, avoid interference, and simplify maintenance.

---

### 4. Results and Conclusions

The **Meridian battery pack** was delivered as a fully integrated mechanical solution, ready for assembly and testing:

* **Key Competency:** Demonstrated ability to **integrate multiple subsystems** within tight mechanical and electrical constraints.
* **Outcome:** Achieved a reliable, maintainable, and mechanically robust battery design.
